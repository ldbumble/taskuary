# Retail & e-commerce

For retail operators, merchants and e-commerce teams selling through stores, websites, apps and marketplaces. This pack covers common retail language, the software behind the work, major market participants and the recurring calendar.

**With this pack, your agents know:**

- **The language.** SKU, GMV, AOV, conversion, sell-through, gross margin, inventory turnover and omnichannel. They read a subject line full of retail metrics the way your team does. → `glossary.md`
- **The software.** Which POS, e-commerce, inventory, order management, payment and customer-service systems teams run, and what each is for. → `software.md`
- **The big players.** Retailers, marketplaces, commerce platforms, associations and regulators. → `players.md`
- **How the year runs.** Daily sales and stock checks, month-end close, seasonal promotions, holiday peaks, inventory planning and returns. → `conventions.md`

It is reference, not advice. Agents use it to understand and to look things up. Retail practices vary by country, channel and product category, so for anything with a date, an amount or a legal or accounting consequence, an agent checks the current official source or company policy before acting on it.
