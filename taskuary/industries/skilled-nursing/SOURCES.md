# Sources - skilled nursing & long-term care

Written for Taskuary as general industry reference. No text was copied from a source whose licence does not allow it.

Facts were drawn from public US federal material (public domain), including:
- CMS: the SNF PPS / PDPM pages, the MDS 3.0 RAI Manual, the State Operations Manual Appendix PP (the F-tags), the
  Payroll-Based Journal pages and the Care Compare / Five-Star technical users' guide.
- HHS OIG reports on nursing homes.

Company names are listed only to describe the market. They imply no endorsement or relationship, and the market changes:
check a company's current status before relying on it.

To verify before each release: rounded market figures, PBJ and cost-report deadlines, and which state Medicaid programs
use RUG-based or PDPM-based case mix.
