# Sources - retail & e-commerce

Written for Taskuary as general industry reference. The definitions and explanations in this pack are written in original wording. No vendor or paywalled text has been pasted in.

## Industry scope and statistics

- **U.S. Census Bureau - North American Industry Classification System (NAICS)**: https://www.census.gov/naics/
  - Used to check the broad U.S. industry scope. NAICS sector 44-45 is Retail Trade. This pack is a practical overview, not a complete NAICS taxonomy.
- **U.S. Census Bureau - Quarterly Retail E-Commerce Sales**: https://www.census.gov/retail/mrts/www/data/pdf/ec_current.pdf
  - Official source for U.S. retail e-commerce statistics. Check the latest release before using figures.

## Retail calendars

- **National Retail Federation - 4-5-4 Calendar**: https://nrf.com/resources/4-5-4-calendar
  - Reference for the 4-5-4 retail calendar. NRF describes it as a voluntary guide; retailers may use other calendars.

## Product identification and occupational context

- **GS1 US**: https://www.gs1us.org/
  - Reference for product identification and supply-chain standards.
- **O*NET Resource Center - O*NET Database**: https://www.onetcenter.org/database.html
  - Potential reference for occupational technology skills. This pack's software table is a general overview, not a direct extraction of O*NET data.

## U.S. public agencies

- **Federal Trade Commission (FTC)**: https://www.ftc.gov/
- **U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA)**: https://www.fda.gov/
- **U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC)**: https://www.cpsc.gov/
- **Internal Revenue Service (IRS)**: https://www.irs.gov/
- **U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP)**: https://www.cbp.gov/

These are official starting points for information within each agency's remit. Applicable rules depend on the product, transaction and jurisdiction.
