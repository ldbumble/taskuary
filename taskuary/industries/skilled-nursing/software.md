# Software landscape - skilled nursing & long-term care

What facilities and operators run, by job. A product named in mail is usually one of these. Vendors merge and rename
often in this market, so treat the lists as "commonly seen", not complete.

| Job | What it does | Commonly seen |
|---|---|---|
| **EHR / clinical record** | Resident record, orders, eMAR, care plans, MDS, census. Usually the system of record for the whole building | PointClickCare, MatrixCare, Netsmart, American HealthTech (part of PointClickCare) |
| **MDS & PDPM analytics** | MDS accuracy, PDPM capture, case-mix and QM previews before submission | Built into the EHR; also SimpleLTC and other MDS/PBJ tools |
| **Therapy** | Rehab documentation, minutes, therapy billing. Often run by a contract therapy company | Net Health (Optima), Casamba |
| **Pharmacy** | Long-term care pharmacy services, dispensing, med management | Omnicare (CVS), PharMerica, and regional LTC pharmacies |
| **Staffing & scheduling** | Shift scheduling, open-shift filling, agency use, HPPD against budget | OnShift, SmartLinx, ShiftMed, IntelyCare |
| **Payroll, HR & timekeeping** | Pay, time punches, benefits - and the source of PBJ hours | ADP, UKG, Paycor, Paylocity |
| **PBJ & staffing compliance** | Builds the quarterly PBJ file from payroll and timekeeping, tracks HPPD and turnover | Payroll and HR vendors' PBJ modules, SimpleLTC |
| **Billing & revenue cycle** | Claims, triple check, collections, Medicaid pending, AR | The EHR's billing module plus RCM services and clearinghouses |
| **Accounting & finance** | GL, AP, consolidations across facilities, PPD reporting | Sage Intacct, NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics, MIP (nonprofits) |
| **Spend & purchasing** | Supplies, purchasing cards, vendor spend | Purchasing platforms and card programs |
| **Care transitions & referrals** | Hospital referrals into the building, readmission alerts | Referral platforms and hospital network tools |
| **Compliance & training** | Staff education, policy, survey readiness, QAPI | Relias, CEU and LMS platforms |
| **Infection control & clinical surveillance** | Infection tracking, antibiotic stewardship | Modules in the EHR, standalone surveillance tools |
| **Nurse call & resident safety** | Call lights, wander management, fall alerts | Facility-installed systems |
| **BI & reporting** | Census, payer mix, PPD and quality dashboards across facilities | Power BI, Tableau, vendor dashboards, a SQL data warehouse |

## Government systems the mail mentions
- **iQIES** - where MDS assessments are submitted; CMS's quality system.
- **PBJ submission** - through CMS's PBJ system.
- **PECOS / CMS-855A** - Medicare enrolment and changes of ownership.
- **Care Compare** - the public Five-Star site.
- **State Medicaid portals** - eligibility, claims and cost report filing. They differ by state.
