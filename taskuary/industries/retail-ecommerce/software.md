# Software landscape - retail & e-commerce

What retail and e-commerce teams run, by job. A product named in mail is usually one of these. Vendors change products and capabilities often, so treat the lists as "commonly seen", not complete.

| Job | What it does | Commonly seen |
|---|---|---|
| **Store checkout / POS** | Records sales, payments, discounts, receipts and store transactions | Square, Shopify POS, Lightspeed and other POS systems |
| **Online shop** | Hosts product pages, carts, checkout and online orders | Shopify, Adobe Commerce, BigCommerce, WooCommerce |
| **Marketplace selling** | Manages seller listings, orders and marketplace operations | Amazon Seller Central, eBay Seller Hub, Walmart Marketplace seller tools |
| **Inventory and purchasing** | Tracks stock, purchase orders, receipts and replenishment | Inventory modules in ERP systems and specialist inventory tools |
| **Order management** | Routes orders across channels, stores, warehouses and fulfilment partners | Order management systems (OMS), sometimes part of a commerce platform |
| **Product information** | Maintains product descriptions, attributes, images and channel data | Product information management (PIM) systems |
| **Merchandising and planning** | Supports assortment, pricing, demand forecasts and buying plans | Retail planning and merchandising systems |
| **Payments** | Connects checkout to payment processing and transaction handling | Stripe, Adyen, PayPal and payment services built into commerce platforms |
| **Customer marketing / CRM** | Manages customer records, campaigns and customer relationships | Salesforce, HubSpot and marketing automation tools |
| **Customer support** | Organises enquiries, returns and service cases | Zendesk, Freshdesk and help-desk tools |
| **Warehouse operations** | Supports receiving, picking, packing and shipping | Warehouse management systems (WMS) |
| **Shipping and delivery** | Compares shipping options, creates labels and tracks parcels | Carrier portals and shipping-management platforms |
| **Accounting and finance** | Records transactions and supports financial reporting | QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite and other accounting or ERP systems |
| **BI and reporting** | Combines data into dashboards and reports for sales, inventory and operations | Power BI, Tableau, spreadsheets and platform analytics |

## Common data and channel systems
- **Web analytics** - records traffic, sessions, conversion events and campaign performance.
- **Payment processor** - authorises and processes payments; it is related to, but not always the same as, a payment gateway.
- **Carrier tracking portals** - show shipment status, delivery exceptions and proof of delivery.

A business may use one integrated platform for several jobs or separate systems connected by data feeds. Product names are examples, not claims that every retailer uses them or that every plan includes the same features.
