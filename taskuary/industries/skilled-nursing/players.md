# Big players - skilled nursing & long-term care

## The market, roughly
- About **15,000** Medicare- and/or Medicaid-certified nursing homes in the US, with about **1.2 million** residents on a
  given day. Most are for-profit, and many belong to multi-facility chains.
- **Medicaid** pays for most resident days (long-stay). **Medicare Part A** pays for fewer days at much higher rates
  (short-stay rehab). The skilled mix is where most margin comes from.
- Margins are thin and labour is the largest cost, so staffing (HPPD, agency use, turnover) drives the economics.

These figures are rounded and move year to year; check CMS and the industry associations for current ones before quoting
them.

## Large operators (examples)
- **The Ensign Group** - a large publicly traded operator of skilled nursing and senior living.
- **PACS Group** - a large skilled nursing operator, publicly listed since 2024.
- **Genesis HealthCare** - historically one of the largest; it has shrunk through divestitures.
- **Life Care Centers of America** - a large privately held operator.
- **National HealthCare Corporation (NHC)** - a long-established publicly traded operator.
- Many regional chains and single-building owners make up the rest. Ownership often splits between a PropCo and an OpCo.

## Real estate and capital
- **Healthcare REITs** own many buildings and lease them to operators, often triple-net: **Omega Healthcare Investors**,
  **Sabra Health Care REIT**, **CareTrust REIT**, and **Welltower** (mostly senior housing).
- **HUD 232** loans, bank debt and private equity finance much of the rest.

## Associations
- **AHCA / NCAL** - American Health Care Association / National Center for Assisted Living: the largest trade
  association.
- **LeadingAge** - the association for nonprofit aging-services providers.
- State affiliates of both run state-level advocacy and Medicaid rate negotiations.

## Regulators and payers
- **CMS** (Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services) - sets the federal requirements of participation, Medicare payment
  (PDPM), Five-Star and the quality programs.
- **State survey agencies** - inspect on CMS's behalf and license facilities.
- **State Medicaid agencies** - set Medicaid rates, case-mix rules, provider taxes and bed-hold policy.
- **HHS OIG** - fraud, abuse and audit oversight.
- **MACs** - process Medicare claims by region.
- **Medicare Advantage plans** - a growing share of short-stay admissions, with their own authorisations and rates.
