# Big players - retail & e-commerce

## The market, roughly
Retail includes store-based and nonstore selling, including e-commerce. In the US NAICS classification, Retail Trade is sector 44-45. The mix of retailers, channels and product categories differs by country and market.

Market shares and sales rankings change; check current company filings and official statistics before quoting a number.

## Retailers and brands (examples)
- **Walmart** - large general-merchandise retailer operating stores and e-commerce channels.
- **Target** - general-merchandise retailer with stores and online sales.
- **Costco** - membership-based warehouse retailer.
- **The Home Depot** - home-improvement retailer.
- **Best Buy** - consumer-electronics retailer.
- **Kroger** - grocery retailer.
- **Nike** - athletic footwear and apparel brand selling through wholesale and direct channels.

## Marketplaces and commerce platforms
- **Amazon** - e-commerce marketplace and retailer; also provides seller services.
- **eBay** - marketplace with listings from businesses and individuals.
- **Etsy** - marketplace known for handmade, vintage and craft-related goods.
- **Walmart Marketplace** - third-party seller marketplace associated with Walmart.
- **Shopify** - commerce platform used by merchants to operate online and other sales channels.
- **Adobe Commerce** - e-commerce platform offering.
- **BigCommerce** - e-commerce platform.

## Associations and standards
- **NRF** - National Retail Federation, a U.S. retail trade association that publishes industry research and a retail 4-5-4 calendar guide.
- **RILA** - Retail Industry Leaders Association, a U.S. trade association representing large retailers.
- **GS1 US** - organisation supporting standards used for product identification and supply-chain information.
- **O*NET Resource Center** - provides occupational information, including information about technology skills and tools associated with occupations.

## Regulators and public agencies (United States)
- **U.S. Census Bureau** - publishes official retail trade and e-commerce statistics and maintains NAICS classifications.
- **FTC** - Federal Trade Commission; consumer protection and competition matters, including some online advertising and commerce issues.
- **FDA** - Food and Drug Administration; regulates specified products, including many foods, drugs and medical products. Scope depends on the product.
- **CPSC** - Consumer Product Safety Commission; consumer product safety within its statutory scope.
- **IRS** - Internal Revenue Service; administers federal tax law.
- **CBP** - U.S. Customs and Border Protection; customs and import requirements.

The relevant regulator depends on the country, product, claim and sales channel. A name appearing here does not mean every retailer is regulated by that agency in the same way.
