# Skilled nursing & long-term care

For operators of skilled nursing facilities (SNFs), nursing homes and post-acute care in the US. These are facilities paid
mostly by Medicare Part A, Medicaid, managed care and private pay, and inspected by state survey agencies on CMS's behalf.

**With this pack, your agents know:**

- **The language.** Census, PPD and HPPD, PDPM and the MDS, payer mix, F-tags and the 2567, PBJ, triple check. They read
  a subject line full of acronyms the way your staff do. → `glossary.md`
- **The software.** Which EHR, therapy, pharmacy, staffing, billing and compliance products facilities run, and what each
  is for. → `software.md`
- **The big players.** The largest operators, the REITs that own the buildings, the associations and the regulators.
  → `players.md`
- **How the year runs.** Month-end census close, triple check, MDS and PBJ deadlines, survey cycles, cost reports and
  changes of ownership. → `conventions.md`

It is reference, not advice. Agents use it to understand and to look things up. Regulations change, so for anything with
a date, an amount or a legal consequence, an agent checks the current CMS or state source before acting on it.
