# How the work runs - skilled nursing & long-term care

## Every day
- **Midnight census** - the day's census is final after midnight. Admissions, discharges, hospital transfers and bed holds
  must be entered right, because billing and PPD are built on it.
- **Morning meeting / stand-up** - the IDT reviews new admissions, changes in condition, falls, incidents and
  hospitalisations.
- **Staffing** - open shifts, call-offs and agency fills are managed against the day's census and the HPPD target.

## Every month
- **Month-end census close** - census, payer days and bed holds are reconciled before billing.
- **Triple check** - billing, MDS and therapy reconcile every Medicare and managed care claim before it is sent.
- **Billing run** - Medicare, Medicaid, managed care and private pay statements go out early in the month.
- **Financial close** - revenue and costs by facility, expressed PPD and against budget, with payer mix and census trends.
- **QAPI / QAA meeting** - usually monthly or quarterly: quality measures, falls, infections, readmissions.

## Every quarter
- **PBJ submission** - staffing hours for the quarter are due to CMS 45 days after it ends. Missing or late data hurts
  the staffing star rating.
- **Medicaid case-mix picture dates** - in states that use them, the MDS on file on the picture date sets the next
  period's Medicaid rate.
- **Five-Star refresh** - CMS updates the ratings on a regular cycle. Staffing and QM changes show up there.

## Every year
- **Standard survey** - unannounced, roughly every 9-15 months, with a statewide average of about 12. Facilities keep a
  survey-readiness routine all year.
- **Cost reports** - the Medicare cost report is due five months after fiscal year end; state Medicaid cost reports
  follow each state's schedule.
- **Medicare rate update** - CMS's SNF PPS final rule comes out each summer and takes effect on Oct 1.
- **Budget** - built on census, payer mix and labour PPD targets.

## Events that drive a lot of mail
- **A survey in the building** - all-hands. The 2567 arrives afterwards, and the POC is due within a short window (often
  10 calendar days).
- **Immediate Jeopardy** - escalates to leadership, legal and regional operations at once.
- **CHOW / acquisition or divestiture** - months of licence, Medicare enrolment, vendor and payroll cutovers. Every
  system (EHR, payroll, bank, PBJ) has to move to the new operator on a set date.
- **Medicare audits (ADR / TPE)** - documentation requests with hard deadlines.
- **Staffing or agency spikes** - watched weekly as agency cost PPD.

## How to read the mail
- A subject with a facility name and a number is usually census, a payer report or a PPD report.
- "Resident" mail involves protected health information (PHI). Treat it as confidential, and never forward or summarise
  it outside the people already on it.
- A vendor alert from the EHR or payroll system about a failed import, a feed or PBJ is operational and time-sensitive.
- Anything from a state agency or CMS with a deadline is a task, not an FYI.

Regulations change. Before acting on a deadline, amount or rule, check the current CMS or state source.
