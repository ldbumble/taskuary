# Glossary - skilled nursing & long-term care

## Census, volume and money
- **Census** - how many residents are in the building, counted daily (the "midnight census"). The number most other
  numbers are divided by.
- **ADC** - average daily census over a period.
- **Occupancy** - census ÷ licensed or operational beds.
- **PPD** - per patient day: a cost or revenue divided by resident days ("nursing cost PPD", "revenue PPD").
- **Patient days / resident days** - the sum of daily census over a period. It is the denominator for PPD.
- **HPPD / HPRD** - (nursing) hours per patient (resident) day: staff hours divided by resident days. It is the core
  staffing measure, and CMS publishes it from PBJ data.
- **Payer mix** - the share of days or revenue by payer: Medicare Part A, Medicaid, managed care / Medicare Advantage,
  private pay, hospice and VA.
- **Skilled mix** - the share of days that are skilled (Medicare A plus managed care) and not long-term custodial. A higher
  skilled mix usually means a higher rate.
- **Private pay** - a resident or family paying out of pocket. Usually the highest daily rate.
- **Bed hold** - payment that keeps a bed while a resident is in hospital or on leave. The rules are set by each state's
  Medicaid program.
- **LOS / ALOS** - (average) length of stay, short-stay (rehab) vs long-stay.
- **Admissions / discharges / readmissions** - a readmission back to hospital within a window (often 30 days) is tracked
  by CMS and by hospital partners.

## Medicare Part A and PDPM
- **Part A (SNF benefit)** - Medicare pays for up to 100 days of skilled care per benefit period after a qualifying stay.
  Days 1-20 are fully covered; days 21-100 carry a daily coinsurance.
- **Qualifying stay / 3-day stay** - a hospital inpatient stay of 3 or more midnights, needed before Part A SNF coverage.
  Waived in some programs and in many Medicare Advantage plans.
- **Benefit period / spell of illness** - starts at admission and ends after 60 days in a row with no hospital or skilled
  care.
- **PDPM** - Patient Driven Payment Model, the Medicare Part A payment system for SNFs since Oct 2019. It replaced RUG-IV.
  The daily rate is built from case-mix components: **PT**, **OT**, **SLP**, **Nursing**, **NTA** (non-therapy
  ancillaries) and a non-case-mix component. The PT, OT and NTA components step down over the stay (the **variable per
  diem adjustment**).
- **HIPPS code** - the 5-character code on the claim that carries the PDPM classification.
- **CMI** - case mix index: a weight for how resource-intensive the residents are. It applies to Medicare and to many
  states' Medicaid rates.
- **RUG-IV / RUGs** - the old Medicare classification before PDPM. Some state Medicaid programs still use RUG-based
  systems, though many have moved to PDPM-based ones.
- **Medicaid case mix** - a state Medicaid rate adjusted by its residents' acuity, often from MDS data captured on a
  "picture date".

## The MDS and assessments
- **MDS 3.0** - Minimum Data Set: the federally required assessment of every resident. It drives payment (PDPM, case mix),
  quality measures and the care plan. Instructions are in the **RAI Manual**.
- **RAI** - Resident Assessment Instrument: the MDS plus the Care Area Assessments and care planning.
- **ARD** - assessment reference date: the last day of the look-back period an MDS describes.
- **5-day / PPS assessment** - the Medicare admission assessment that sets the PDPM classification.
- **IPA** - Interim Payment Assessment: optional, used when a resident's condition changes the classification.
- **OBRA assessments** - the federally required clinical assessments (admission, quarterly, annual, significant change),
  apart from the payment ones.
- **SCSA / SCPA** - significant change in status / significant correction to prior assessment.
- **PPS discharge / Part A discharge** - the assessment that closes a Part A stay.
- **MDS coordinator** - the nurse who schedules, completes and submits MDS assessments. Payment accuracy depends on this
  role.
- **iQIES** - CMS's system where MDS assessments are submitted.
- **ADL** - activities of daily living (bed mobility, transfer, eating, toileting and so on). Scored in the MDS and drives
  acuity.
- **Section GG** - the MDS functional items (self-care and mobility) used in PDPM and quality reporting.

## Survey, quality and compliance
- **Survey** - the unannounced state inspection on CMS's behalf: the **standard (recertification) survey**, plus
  **complaint** and **life safety code** surveys.
- **F-tag** - a numbered federal requirement (for example F689 accidents, F880 infection control). A deficiency is cited
  by F-tag.
- **Scope and severity** - the A-L grid rating each deficiency. **J, K, L** are Immediate Jeopardy.
- **IJ** - Immediate Jeopardy: a deficiency that caused, or is likely to cause, serious harm. Escalates fast and can bring
  heavy penalties.
- **CMS-2567** - the statement of deficiencies the facility receives after a survey.
- **POC** - plan of correction: the facility's written response to the 2567, with dates.
- **CMP** - civil money penalty.
- **DPNA** - denial of payment for new admissions, a remedy CMS can impose.
- **SFF** - Special Focus Facility: a program for persistently poor performers, with more frequent surveys.
- **Five-Star / Care Compare** - CMS's public star rating, with three domains: health inspections, staffing (from PBJ) and
  quality measures (QMs).
- **QM** - quality measure, calculated from MDS and claims data. Short-stay and long-stay measures are kept apart.
- **QAPI** - quality assurance and performance improvement: the required program and its committee (QAA).
- **SNF QRP** - Quality Reporting Program. Not reporting cuts the annual Medicare update.
- **SNF VBP** - Value-Based Purchasing: a Medicare payment adjustment based on quality measures such as readmissions.
- **PBJ** - Payroll-Based Journal: quarterly submission to CMS of staff hours from payroll and timekeeping. It feeds
  HPPD, the staffing star and public turnover data.
- **CHOW** - change of ownership: a new operator takes over a licence or Medicare provider agreement. A slow, paperwork-
  heavy process with CMS (CMS-855A) and the state.

## Billing and revenue cycle
- **Triple check** - the monthly pre-billing review in which billing, MDS/nursing and therapy reconcile each Medicare (and
  often managed care) claim before it goes out.
- **UB-04 / 837I** - the institutional claim form and its electronic version.
- **MAC** - Medicare Administrative Contractor: the company that processes a region's Medicare claims.
- **ADR / TPE / RAC** - additional documentation request, targeted probe-and-educate, recovery audit contractor: kinds
  of Medicare review and audit.
- **Medicaid pending** - a resident whose Medicaid application is not yet approved, so the days go unpaid.
- **Cost report** - the annual Medicare cost report (CMS-2540-10 for SNFs), plus state Medicaid cost reports. They drive
  rates in many states.
- **Provider tax / QAF** - a state assessment on nursing homes, usually per non-Medicare patient day, that funds the
  state's share of Medicaid.

## People and roles
- **NHA / Administrator** - the licensed nursing home administrator who runs the building.
- **DON / ADON** - director of nursing / assistant director of nursing.
- **RN, LPN/LVN, CNA** - registered nurse, licensed practical (vocational) nurse, certified nursing assistant.
- **Medical director** - the physician responsible for medical care policy in the facility.
- **IDT** - the interdisciplinary team: nursing, therapy, dietary, social services, activities and MDS.
- **Regional / VP of operations** - an operator's multi-facility management layer.
- **Agency staff** - nurses and aides from staffing agencies. Expensive, and closely watched as agency cost PPD.

## Settings and structures
- **SNF / NF** - a skilled nursing facility (Medicare certified) / a nursing facility (Medicaid certified). Most buildings
  are both, "dually certified".
- **LTC** - long-term care. In casual use it also means the custodial, long-stay side as opposed to short-stay rehab.
- **Post-acute** - care after a hospital stay: SNF, home health, inpatient rehab (IRF), long-term acute care hospital
  (LTACH).
- **ALF / AL, IL, MC** - assisted living, independent living, memory care: senior housing, licensed by the state, mostly
  not Medicare.
- **CCRC / life plan community** - a campus offering IL, AL and skilled nursing.
- **PropCo / OpCo** - the real-estate company that owns the building and the operating company that runs the licence,
  often joined by a lease.
- **Triple-net (NNN) lease** - a lease where the operator pays taxes, insurance and maintenance. Common between REITs and
  operators.
- **HUD 232** - HUD's mortgage insurance program for nursing homes and assisted living.
- **I-SNP** - Institutional Special Needs Plan: a Medicare Advantage plan for long-stay nursing home residents.
