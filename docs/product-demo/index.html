Start with “Walk me through my tasks”
Start with Walk me through my tasks. Here's Ruth's request.

Connect the tools you already use
First, connect the tools you already use. Outlook, Gmail, Teams, and other services can bring requests into Taskuary. You set this up once. You do not need to copy each new request into an AI chat.

Connect the source of your numbers
Then connect the source of your information. In this example, the numbers belong in the company's finance database. Connections also include spreadsheets and business systems. Your assistant works from the sources you make available.

Choose the AI you want to use
Choose the AI connection for the work. You can configure Claude Code or Codex through their command line connections, or connect a local model through Ollama. After setup, the everyday work happens in the same general assistant workspace.

Open the request. The assistant gets to work.
Now open Ruth's request and choose Send to agent. Her original question goes straight into the general assistant workspace. In this scripted example, the assistant prepares the August total, compares it with July, and checks that the categories add up.

The answer is ready
Here is the result: one hundred ninety two thousand, six hundred dollars, up eight percent from July. The category breakdown is included, along with the reporting cutoff and what was excluded. The assistant has also prepared the reply.

Review the finished reply
Open Review. The reply to Ruth is ready to read, with the same numbers and the important context. You can edit the wording here. You do not have to assemble the answer or copy it back into your email.

One approval closes the loop
When it looks right, approve the reply. It leaves the pending queue. This demo sends nothing; it shows the approval step. That is the everyday flow: a request comes in, your general assistant prepares the work, and you review the result.
